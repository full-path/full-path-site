Produced for Age Friendly Arizona and the Maricopa Association of Governments (MAG) in March 2020, this report assessed technology needs and options for a rural transportation incubator program serving rural and tribal communities in Arizona.

Rural transportation incubators are programs designed to help small, emerging transportation providers — often volunteer-based or community-run — develop the organizational and operational capacity to serve their communities sustainably. Technology is one piece of that, but only one piece. The report examined what technology infrastructure would actually be useful and viable for providers at that early stage of development, rather than what a mature transit agency might use.

The assessment covered scheduling and dispatch tools, data collection and reporting requirements, coordination platforms, and the technology literacy and support needs of participating organizations. Recommendations were deliberately conservative: tools that could be adopted incrementally and maintained without dedicated IT staff.
