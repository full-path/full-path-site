CareOregon is a coordinated care organization serving Oregon Health Plan members in the Portland metro area, and its Ride to Care program coordinates non-emergency medical transportation (NEMT) for those members. The program depends on a network of partner organizations and technology systems working together — a structure that was stood up quickly to fill a sudden gap in NEMT coverage and had been operating on fast decisions ever since, without the chance to step back and take stock. CareOregon engaged Full Path, working alongside Garnet Consulting, to take that structured look at Ride to Care's technology environment and help chart a path forward.

The engagement ran in two phases across the fall of 2023. The first produced a Current State Roadmap: an inventory of the software systems, workflows, and data flows involved in the trip lifecycle from request to completed ride, along with an assessment of the technology capabilities of CareOregon staff, partner agencies, and transportation providers. The second phase built on that foundation with a Future State Roadmap, laying out recommended next steps for the program's technology and organizational capacity.

The work is representative of how Full Path approaches technology decisions generally — assessment before recommendation, and organizational capacity considered alongside software capability — applied here to a more complex environment than a single small transit agency: a coordinated care organization managing NEMT service delivery through multiple partner organizations and vendors at once.
