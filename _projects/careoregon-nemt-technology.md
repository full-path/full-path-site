## Background

CareOregon is a coordinated care organization providing Medicaid insurance benefits to Oregon Health Plan members in the Portland metro area. Its Ride to Care program provides non-emergency medical transportation (NEMT) for those members. The program is operated by a network of three partner organizations and several technology systems working together. This structure was stood up quickly to fill a sudden gap in NEMT service.

## The Problem

Due to its rapid implementation, Ride to Care was operating without a strong technology foundation. The partner organizations and CareOregon experienced difficulties with their software and communicating necessary information throughout the system. CareOregon hired Full Path and Garnet Consulting to take a structured look at Ride to Care’s technology environment and help chart a path forward.

## The Solution

Through a combination of virtual meetings, on-site shadowing, and one-on-one interviews, the Full Path team was able to develop a detailed and comprehensive understanding of the Ride to Care system.

The project ran in two phases. The first produced a Current State Roadmap, an inventory of the software systems, workflows, and data flows involved in the trip lifecycle from request to completed ride, along with an assessment of the technology capabilities of CareOregon staff, partner agencies, and transportation providers.

The second phase built on that foundation with a Future State Roadmap, laying out recommended next steps for the program’s technology and supporting organizational capacity.

Project success relied on the Full Path team having a deep understanding of demand-responsive transportation operations, domain-specific software applications, data management best practices, and effective outreach and engagement with a diverse set of stakeholders.

## The Outcome

In the end, the two roadmaps improved understanding within the Ride to Care network and helped CareOregon leadership better understand the complexity of NEMT as a whole.

This work is representative of how Full Path approaches technology assessments generally: thorough, on-the-ground engagement with the people doing the work of providing transportation, a focus on building organizational capacity alongside technology capability, and to-the-point deliverables that tell the story of where an organization stands and what concrete steps will help it move toward its goals.
