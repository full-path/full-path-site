## Background

CareOregon is a coordinated care organization providing Medicade insurance benefits to Oregon Health Plan members in the Portland metro area. Its Ride to Care program provides non-emergency medical transportation (NEMT) for those members. The program is operated by a network of three partner organizations and several technology systems working together — a structure that was stood up quickly to fill a sudden gap in NEMT service.

## The Problem

CareOregon hired Full Path and Garnet Consulting to take a structured look at Ride to Care's technology environment and help chart a path forward.

## The Solution

The project ran in two phases. The first produced a Current State Roadmap, an inventory of the software systems, workflows, and data flows involved in the trip lifecycle from request to completed ride, along with an assessment of the technology capabilities of CareOregon staff, partner agencies, and transportation providers. The second phase built on that foundation with a Future State Roadmap, laying out recommended next steps for the program's technology and supporting organizational capacity.


Project success relied on the Full Path team having a deep understanding of demand-responsive transportation operations, domain-specific software applications, data management best practices, and effective outreach and engagement with a diverse set of stakeholders. In the end, the two roadmaps improved understanding within the Ride to Care network and helped CareOregon leadership better understand the complexity of NEMT as a whole.

## The Outcome

Applied in this case to a more complex, multi-organization environment, the work is representative of how Full Path approaches technology assessments generally: thorough, on-the-ground engagement with the people doing the work of providing transportation, a focus on building organizational capacity alongside technology capability, and to-the-point deliverables that tell the story of where an organization stands and what concrete steps will help it move toward its goals.
