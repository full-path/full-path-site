This report documents the development of RideSheet and its early deployment with two agencies in rural Lake County, Oregon: Inner Court Family Center and the Lake County Senior Center Association.

The report makes the case that small rural transportation providers — the ones serving older adults and people with disabilities in communities where no commercial software is viable — can benefit from purpose-built, open-source coordination tools. It details the problem RideSheet was designed to solve, the development process, and the outcomes observed in the pilot.

RideSheet was initially funded by the AARP Public Policy Institute specifically to address the gap between the scheduling and coordination tools available to large urban transit systems and what small rural providers could actually afford and operate.
