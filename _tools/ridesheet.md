RideSheet is a free, open-source scheduling and trip coordination system for small and rural demand-responsive transportation providers — the agencies that typically have no dedicated IT staff, limited broadband, and no budget for expensive proprietary software.

Built on Google Sheets and Google Apps Script, RideSheet handles the full data lifecycle from the moment a person requests a ride through end-of-month reporting. It tracks every key data element: customers, trips, runs, drivers, vehicles, and common addresses.

## Who Uses It

It was initially funded by the AARP Public Policy Institute and piloted with two agencies in Lake County, Oregon. Currently, RideSheet is in active use across 4 states, with new agencies being added regularly.
