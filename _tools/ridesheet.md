RideSheet is a free, open-source scheduling and trip coordination system for small and rural demand-responsive transportation providers — the agencies that typically have no dedicated IT staff, limited broadband, and no budget for expensive proprietary software.

Built on Google Sheets and Google Apps Script, RideSheet handles the full data lifecycle from scheduling through completed-trip reporting. It tracks customers, trips, and runs; supports inter-agency coordination via a standardized API based on the TCRP Report 210 Transactional Data Specification; and is designed to be maintained and understood by the organizations using it.

## Who Uses It

As of early 2026, RideSheet is in active use at 7 agencies in 4 states. It was initially funded by the AARP Public Policy Institute and piloted with two agencies in Lake County, Oregon: Inner Court Family Center and the Lake County Senior Center Association.

**Development and support:**
- Software development: Garnet Consulting
- Strategic support: National Rural Transit Assistance Program (NRTAP)
