Published by the National Center for Applied Transit Technology (N-CATT), this whitepaper offers a structured, capacity-building-first approach to technology decision-making for transit agencies. Co-authored with Lara Bjork, it was originally published in February 2022 and substantially revised in April 2025.

The framework is grounded in systems thinking and design thinking. It gives agencies a repeatable process for evaluating and selecting technology — one that starts with organizational readiness and internal clarity rather than vendor selection. The underlying argument is that most technology failures in community transportation aren't technology failures at all: they're organizational and process failures that technology can't fix.

## What It Covers

- How to define the problem before evaluating solutions
- Assessing organizational capacity and readiness before committing to a technology
- Structured approaches to vendor evaluation and selection
- Building internal ownership so technology actually gets used after implementation

## Who It's For

Small and rural transit agencies, state DOTs with transit oversight responsibilities, and mobility management programs evaluating new tools or systems. It's written for non-technical managers who need a way to think clearly about technology decisions, not a checklist of features.
